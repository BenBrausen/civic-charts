Use Civic Charts in your AI assistant

Free, no account, read-only.

Connect your AI tool and it can look up the latest official U.S. numbers (jobs, prices, rent, gas, wages, health, climate, elections) for the country, any state or any county. Answers come with the source and a link to the chart, so you can check the data or share it.

Try asking: "What's the unemployment rate in Texas?", "How has rent changed in Hennepin County?", "What new government data came out this week?"

Perplexity and other AI tools

Any tool that supports MCP connectors can use the address above. Look for "connectors", "MCP" or "custom tools" in its settings.

What it can do

  • Search charts by question, in plain words.
  • Get a chart: headline, key facts, latest value and date, how to read it, source link, chart link and preview image. Ask about one state or county for its value and rank.
  • Newest releases: what official data came out most recently.

Every number comes from the publisher's data, and chart text is checked against it. For crawlers and developers: /ai/index.json (every chart's summary) and /llms.txt.
