Using Civic Charts

Civic Charts is free to use. Charts, images, videos and text you make with it are yours to share and publish, including commercially. Keep the source line on charts you publish, so readers can see where the numbers come from.

The data

Data comes from the government agencies and research organizations named on each chart, and is shown as they publish it, with any calculations we make explained on the chart or its page. Publishers revise their data, and errors can happen. We check every chart's text against the data before it can be exported, but we can't guarantee that every figure is complete, current or error-free. Check the original source before relying on a number for an important decision.

Spot an error? Email corrections@civiccharts.com. We review every report and correct any errors we find.

Fair use of the service

Don't use Civic Charts to mislead: for example, by editing a chart's text to say something the data doesn't support, or by removing the source to pass a chart off as something it isn't. Don't try to overload the site or get around its limits, such as the voiceover limits.
