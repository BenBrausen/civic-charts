Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Civic Charts turns official public data into charts, maps and videos. It has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name or email. Here is everything it collects and why.

Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and which features are used: for example, which datasets are opened, which sizes are exported, and what people search for, so we know what to improve. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage is off by default. The search words you type are sent so we can see which data people look for and can't find.

Stored in your browser

Your browser keeps your current chart and settings for up to 7 days, so a reload doesn't lose your work, plus a few preferences such as Simple or Advanced mode and your state. This stays on your device; we never receive it. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Data you load or upload

Files you upload are read in your browser and aren't sent to us. When you load a dataset from a government or university portal, the file passes through our server (a Cloudflare Worker) so your browser is allowed to read it. We don't keep it beyond a short cache.

AI voiceovers

If you make an AI voiceover, the script text is sent through our Cloudflare Worker to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, which returns the audio. To avoid paying for the same audio twice, the Worker keeps the audio for that exact script for up to 30 days. Cloudflare Turnstile runs a bot check first, and Cloudflare may process technical signals such as your IP address to do it. The Worker also counts voiceovers per IP address for an hour, to limit abuse.

Our social media accounts

Civic Charts posts its own charts to its accounts on Bluesky, Threads, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Reddit, using those platforms' official tools. That connection is used only to publish Civic Charts' own posts. It doesn't read or collect anyone else's data.

Uploads to the Civic Charts YouTube channel use YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply. The connection can only upload videos to our own channel. It doesn't access, store or share any YouTube user data, and its access can be revoked at any time in Google's security settings. Because the connection stores no YouTube data, there is nothing to delete; revoking access removes it entirely, and you can ask us to delete anything about you by emailing hello@civiccharts.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Who else is involved

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google (Analytics, Text-to-Speech) and Cloudflare (Worker, Turnstile) process data as described above under their own privacy policies. We don't sell data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@civiccharts.com.
